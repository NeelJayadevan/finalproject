Peach Yogurt

Nutrition Facts

The serving size is the whole container. Which is 150g. The yogurt has 110 calories in it. It has 50mg of Sodium and 14g of sugar, 9g of sugar are added sugars. It also has 11g of protein which is 22% of your daily vaule.

Allergens

This yogurt contains milk and peach.

Two reasons why I enjoy this snack

I really like the pieces of peach in this yogurt. The sweet peaches go super well with the sour taste of the greek yogurt.

I like how this yogurt is sweet and isn't as sour as just greek yogurt but also has a lot of protein and natural sugars.
