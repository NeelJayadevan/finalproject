Being a chip, this product is not the healthiest. However, it isn't as unhealthy as other chips such as Doritos and lacks harmful dyes such as Red 40. There are still healthier potato chip options, but if enjoyed sparingly and put in a diet still rich in other foods such as fruits and vegetables, then it can be fine. Two reasons why I enjoy this snack

Tim's is a chips brand based in WA that creates potato chips. This brand may not be as large and widespread as Dorito's, but it is still incredibly tasty--if not even better. There are many appealing Tim's flavors, including the sour cream & onion one and the plain one. However, one flavor that I want to highlight on this page is the jalapeño flavor. This flavor is one that I would recommend getting from the Levinger-Poole Commons during lunch or even during other times, like before-school and after-school.

There are two primary reasons explaining this product's greatness.

Reason 1: The taste

This one may be a bit obvious, but the taste of this chip is impeccable. I will assure you that the jalapeño flavor is not spicy whatsoever, but it is nonetheless satisfying. It is subtle, but brings a big impact that makes it very enjoyable. The subtle nature of the flavor may make it less appealing and vibrant than other chips, but it also helps this flavor blend more seamlessly with other foods.

Reason 2: The chip itself

As I said above, Tim's is a very promising chip brand, and this product perfectly shows that. The satisfying balance between being firm and fall-apart crunchy seen in the chip allows it to be satisfying in a "goldilocks" sort of way, and I would consider this to be one of the best potato chip brands. The primary thing setting potato chips apart from tortilla chips is this feel, but the subtle flavor always in the background is too a factor. This subtle flavor, given the sublety of the jalapeño flavor, crafts an incredibly poignant flavor.

Conclusion

Overall, this is a great product, and despite the comparison being close, I would recommend it over getting Dorito's.
