LPC Snack Guide

Looking for a snack between classes? This website helps students explore the snacks at the LPC and decide what to grab. Browse each snack's page to learn more and discover something new to try. This guide also has the nutrition-facts on each product!

With limited time between classes, choosing a snack should be easy. Our guide brings the options together in one place so students can compare snacks, find their favorites, and make a quick decision before heading to the LPC.
