Choclate Muffin

Nutrition Facts

The serving size is the whole Muffin. Which is 113g. The whole Muffin has 420 calories. It has 610mg of Sodium and 34g of sugar. It also has 3g of fiber and 7g of protein.

Allergens

This Muffin contains egg, milk, soy and wheat. It may contain sesame seeds and tree nuts.

Two reasons why I enjoy this snack

I like this muffin because it's super chocolaty. It has a chocolate base and then has chocolate chips too. I love putting it in the microwave for 30 seconds because the chocolate melts and it taste really good.

I also like this muffin for breakfast if I get to school early because it's super good and I think it's nice to start a day off with something sweet.
