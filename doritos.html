Doritos Nacho Cheese Flavored Tortilla Chips

Nutrition Facts

Nutrition information for a bag of this size

  • Calories: 260.
  • Total fat: 13 grams, 17% of daily value.
  • Saturated fat: 2 grams, 10% of daily value.
  • Trans fat: 0 grams, 0% of daily value.
  • Cholestrol: 0 milligrams, 0% of daily value.
  • Sodium: 290 milligrams, 13% of daily value.
  • Total Carbohydrates: 30 grams, 11% of daily value.
  • Dietary Fiber: 2 grams, 9% of daily value.
  • Total sugars: 1 gram
  • Protein: 4 grams
  • Vitamin D: 0 micrograms, 0% of daily value.
  • Calcium: 80 milligrams, 6% of daily value.
  • Iron: 0.5 milligrams, 2% of daily value.
  • Potassium: 170 milligrams, 2% of daily value.

Ingredients:

Corn, Vegetable Oil [Corn, Canola, Soybean, And/Or Sunflower Oil], Corn Maltodextrin, And Less Than 2% Of Cheddar Cheese [Milk, Cheese Cultures, Salt, Enzymes], Salt, Whey Monosodium Glutamate, Buttermilk, Romano Cheese [Milk, Cheese Cultures, Salt, Enzymes], Whey Protein Concentrate, Onion Powder, Potassium Salt, Corn Flour, Natural & Artificial Flavors, Dextrose, Tomato Powder, Lactose, Spices, Artificial Color [Yellow 6, Yellow 5, Red 40], Lactic Acid, Citric Acid, Sugar, Garlic Powder, Skim Milk, Red And Green Bell Peppers, Disodium Inosinate, And Disodium Guanylate.

Contains Milk Ingredients

As you can see from this information, this is not the healthiest chip in the world. Its amount of cheese is negligible, it has various oils and dyes, and a decently high [but not overboard] quantity of fat and sodium in relation to the daily recommended value. Protein is also negligible, with this only having 4 grams for 260 calories and 13 grams of fat. It is tasty, but it is a good idea to eat it sparingly.

Two reasons why I enjoy this snack

Doritos Nacho Cheese Tortilla Chips are flavorful, satisfying chips that pair with any meal. They have a more vibrant taste than other chips--so you may not want them if you like simpler foods--but if you like nacho cheese, then this is an optimal chip to eat. The cool ranch flavor of Doritos is good too, but I chose to highlight this flavor as it is the best of the two in my opinion.

There are several reasons that explain the appeal of this snack, but there are two main reasons that I will cover.

Reason 1: The taste

I discussed this above, but the taste of these chips is enjoyable. Some people may disagree if they dislike cheese, but I like cheese, and I imagine that many people won't agree. This obviously isn't made with real cheese--so don't use this as a protein source--but use them as an enjoyment source.

Reason 2: The satisfication

This was also mentioned above, but I will go into more detail here. The elaborate taste of these chips, combined with the crunch that is characteristic of tortilla chips, creates a snack that can fit anywhere. It isn't suggested to have this often, since it isn't the healthiest, but an occasional grab allows for enjoyment and health to be balanced.

Conclusion

Overall, this is a great snack--not if you want something healthy, but definitely if you want a salty snack or chip bag to top off your lunch.
